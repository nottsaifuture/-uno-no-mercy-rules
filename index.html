UNO Show 'Em No Mercy Helper

Learn the rules, try the cards, then test yourself! 🎉

How to play in 7 steps

  1. Everyone gets 7 cards. Flip one card from the deck to start the discard pile.
  2. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card by colour, number or symbol. Wild cards can go on anything.
  3. Can't play? Keep drawing until you get a card you can play. (No Mercy!)
  4. Draw cards can be stacked: put down a draw card that's the same or bigger to pass the pain along.
  5. Play a 7: swap hands with any player. Play a 0: everyone passes their hand to the next player.
  6. If you ever have 25 or more cards, the Mercy Rule knocks you out!
  7. Shout "UNO!" when you have one card left. Get rid of all your cards (or be the last player left) to win the round.

Build a stack tower

Tap draw cards to stack them. You can only stack a card that is the same or higher than the top one. The player who can't (or won't) stack draws everything!
